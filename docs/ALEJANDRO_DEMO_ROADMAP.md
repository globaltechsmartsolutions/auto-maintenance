# Alejandro's Demo and Product Roadmap

## Objective

Own the customer-facing quality of the WIAControl demonstration while keeping
technical backend changes coordinated through reviewed work.

## Safe ownership area

Demo script, English copy, sample data, responsive polish, accessibility, route
navigation, screenshots, customer story, and presentation checklists can be
improved independently when their contracts remain stable.

Database schema, migrations, authentication, authorization, API contracts,
billing, webhooks, and assignment policy require coordination and review.

## Phase 1: Demo narrative

Build one concise story: identify a coverage gap, inspect eligible replacements,
record the coordinator's choice, show the employee clocking flow, and resolve an
incident. Define what is real, simulated, and planned.

## Phase 2: Realistic sample data

Use consistent companies, employees, skills, worksites, customers, shifts,
clock events, and incidents. Avoid perfect data: include one absence, one gap,
one late event, and one correction that demonstrate product value.

## Phase 3: Commercial and visual polish

Prioritize `/control`, `/employee`, `/time-tracking`, `/worksites`, `/shifts`,
and `/booking`. Improve hierarchy, action clarity, empty and error states, table
readability, status contrast, and English terminology.

## Phase 4: Responsive review

Test public and employee routes at common phone widths, internal routes at tablet
and desktop widths, keyboard navigation, zoom, long names, and dense data.

## Pre-demo checklist

- Automated quality gate passes.
- Browser console is clean.
- Sample state and roles are correct.
- Desktop and mobile windows are prepared.
- External-service limitations are understood.
- The complete story has been rehearsed without manual data repair.

## Deliverables

Maintain the demo script, local runbook, QA checklist, known limitations, and a
short customer feedback record. A demo is ready when it is repeatable, honest,
visually coherent, and focused on measurable operational value.
